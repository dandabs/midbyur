<Subtitle>A design system for iOS, Android, and web</Subtitle>

I created Miðbýur ("downtown" in Faroese) as my design system for iOS, Android, and web apps. It works across Next.js and Expo (and should work with most React implementations, and just as raw CSS, though that's untested). I really enjoy making apps, but I slow down and struggle when I have to design unique UI for each one — so this is just a thing to make writing UI in my apps easier, and to give them a more solidified feel across everything.

I've taken inspiration from the Digital Iceland, GOV.UK, and NYC Digital design systems. All of the colours are taken from the RGB values of Icelandic road signs.

## Fonts

Miðbýur uses three variable weight "Stack Sans" cuts, plus a monospace fallback stack. `Text` picks one automatically per `variant`, or you can set `fontFamily` directly.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={["14px", "18px", "24px", "32px", "48px"]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Miðbýur — Aa Bb Cc 123"
  fontFamily='"Stack Sans Headline", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif'
/>

**Stack Sans Headline** — used by `h1`, `h1Subheading`, `h2`, `h2Subheading`, `h3`, and `h3Subheading`. Reach for it on primary page and section headings: it's the strongest, most authoritative heading voice in the system.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={["14px", "18px", "24px", "32px", "48px"]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Miðbýur — Aa Bb Cc 123"
  fontFamily='"Stack Sans Notch", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif'
/>

**Stack Sans Notch** — used by `display`, `h4`, `h4Subheading`, `h5`, and `h5Subheading`. A friendlier, more geometric cut for hero/display moments and secondary-level headings.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={["14px", "18px", "24px", "32px", "48px"]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Miðbýur — Aa Bb Cc 123"
  fontFamily='"Stack Sans Text", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif'
/>

**Stack Sans Text** — used by `body`, `bodySm`, `label`, and `caption`. The workhorse: long-form copy, form labels, and captions, tuned to stay legible at small sizes.

<Typeset
  fontSizes={["14px", "18px", "24px", "32px", "48px"]}
  fontWeight={400}
  sampleText="Miðbýur — Aa Bb Cc 123"
  fontFamily="ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, 'Liberation Mono', 'Courier New', monospace"
/>

**Mono** — the system monospace stack, not assigned to any `Text` variant by default. Opt in with `fontFamily="mono"` for inline code, technical values, or anything that should read as literal.
